Privacy Policy

Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy describes how SidePortal (the "Extension") collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Access and Process

To provide its core productivity features, SidePortal requests authorization to access certain data from your Google Account. All Google data is retrieved and processed entirely locally within your browser's extension sandbox. No Google user data is ever transmitted to, or stored on, any external servers.

  • Gmail: The Extension queries your inbox labels (via the Gmail API) solely to calculate and display your unread email count badge in the sidebar UI. No email content or headers are read, stored, or transmitted.
  • Google Calendar: The Extension retrieves your upcoming calendar events to display a schedule preview inside the sidebar. None of your event details are stored or shared.
  • Google Drive: The Extension conducts metadata searches of your files when you input query terms into the sidebar's search bar. Search queries and results are handled entirely client-side.
  • Google Profile Email: Used locally to identify the active connected account in the Extension header.

2. Shared Information (Organization Links)

SidePortal allows users to create or join group workspaces (e.g. for CMEs and Instructors) to share web bookmarks ("Quick Links").

  • Database Storage: Group bookmarks (title and URL) and group memberships are stored securely in our Supabase database instance.
  • Role Separation: Only the group creator (Admin) has the ability to write, modify, or delete group bookmarks. General members have read-only access to bookmarks.
  • No Personal Data Harvesting: We do not collect, harvest, or sell personal group activity logs.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security protocols to protect all stored group bookmarks and database rows. Access is secured using Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring that only authenticated members of a group can view the bookmarks associated with that group.
